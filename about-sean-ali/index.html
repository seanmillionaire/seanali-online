About Sean Ali

Hey, my name is Sean Ali.

I didn’t start with a secret advantage.

I grew up in a single-parent home in Canada. At 15, I was diagnosed with cancer and went through surgery. That experience stayed with me. Life isn’t guaranteed.

As a teenager, I got interested in computers, websites, and selling online. My first sale showed me that I could build something from wherever I lived.

I’ve now spent more than 15 years building online businesses. Along the way, meditation and visualization became practices I kept coming back to.
