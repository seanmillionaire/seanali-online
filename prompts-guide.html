Prompt #1
Turn One Idea Into 10 TikTok Hooks
Perfect first prompt. Use it even if you have no idea what your niche is yet.
I want to make videos about [TOPIC — example: saving money, losing weight, learning a skill]. Give me 10 short hooks (opening lines) for TikTok or Instagram Reels. Make them: - Simple enough for a 5th grader to understand - Sound like a real person, not a marketer - Make someone stop scrolling - Focus on a problem, a mistake, or a surprising fact Example format: Hook 1: Hook 2: Hook 3: (etc.)
🆕 Never posted before? Here's exactly what to do: Replace [TOPIC] with something you know or care about. Even "saving money as a student" or "meals under $10" works. Paste into ChatGPT (free at chat.openai.com). Read the hooks out loud. Pick the one that feels most natural to say.
Your move right now
  • Pick ONE hook — the one you'd actually say out loud
  • Open your phone camera and film yourself saying it
  • Post it to TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube Shorts
  • Done. That's your first piece of content.
⚡ Real result "This exact format pulled 1,200 clicks on a hook about money stress. The ugliest video I ever filmed."
