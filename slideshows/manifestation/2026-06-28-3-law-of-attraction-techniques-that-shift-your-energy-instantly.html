Technique #1

The handled question.

Ask: “How would I move if this was already handled?”

Then choose one calm next step.

05 / 15

Why it works

It pulls attention away from panic.

It puts your mind on identity.

Then it points you toward action.

06 / 15

Neville would call this state.

Neville Goddard

Technique #2

The repetition reset.

Pick one short phrase that implies the new state.

Use it when the old story starts running.
