# Offer Rules

## Main Rule

Sell the idea first.

Product second.

Pain before solution.

Product as the vehicle.

## Offer Flow

1. Name the pain.
2. Name the hidden mechanism.
3. Show the better belief.
4. Give one simple move.
5. Present the offer as the next logical step.

## Never Present Offers Like This

Product name.

Description.

Button.

## Present Offers Like This

You are stuck in a loop.

The problem is not effort.

The problem is the state you keep practicing.

Use this tool tonight to interrupt the loop.

CTA.

## CTA Rules

- One CTA only.
- Match CTA to the pain.
- Do not force the offer.
- The CTA should feel like relief.
- The offer should be the next obvious move.
