# Validation Team

## Content War Room

Before publishing any concept, run it through six validators.

Each validator scores 0-10.

Maximum score: 60.

Publish line: 42/60.

Anything under 42 gets remixed.

Anything that fails Sean's title preference gets remixed even if the score is high.

## 1. MrBeast

Question:

Would millions of people care?

Scores high when:

- Massive curiosity.
- Broad audience.
- Instantly understandable.
- Emotional.
- Clickable.

Scores low when:

- Too smart.
- Too niche.
- Too educational.
- Too soft.

## 2. Alex Hormozi

Question:

Does this create demand?

Scores high when:

- Pain is clear.
- Villain is clear.
- Mechanism is clear.
- Transformation is clear.
- It points toward a buying desire.

## 3. Dan Martell

Question:

Does this build authority?

Scores high when:

- Sean looks experienced.
- It shows operator judgment.
- It attracts quality people.
- It makes the viewer trust Sean more.

## 4. Tai Lopez

Question:

Is this attached to an existing market?

Scores high when:

- People already search it.
- The audience already exists.
- It borrows attention from books, wealth, status, AI, Neville, money, sleep, or success.

## 5. Sean Ali

Question:

Would Sean be proud of this in 5 years?

Scores high when:

- It feels true.
- It helps the viewer.
- It aligns with Sean's mission.
- It does not chase garbage views.

## 6. The Market

Question:

Would a stranger search this today?

Scores high when:

- Search demand exists.
- Trend demand exists.
- Audience size is large.
- The outcome is obvious.

## Required Title Checks

Every title must pass:

- Number.
- Clickbait.
- Outcome.
- Timeframe.
- Reads naturally.
