# Slideshow Style

## Standard Structure

Use 13 slides when possible.

1. Big curiosity title.
2. Hidden problem.
3. Kitchen-table explanation.
4. Sean story beat.
5. Big shift.
6. Why this clicks.
7. 7-minute move.
8. What changes.
9. Sean/Neville frame.
10. One move.
11. Recap.
12. Final question.
13. CTA.

## Slide Rules

- One idea per slide.
- Short text.
- Big type.
- Strong contrast.
- No walls of text.
- Every slide should make them swipe.
- Use tension, not decoration.
- The slideshow must match the actual concept.

## Style

- Direct-response.
- Bold.
- Simple.
- High contrast.
- Green/yellow/black is okay.
- Big promise.
- Big CTA.
- Clear bullets.

## Do Not

- Do not make a thin 5-slide deck.
- Do not use generic filler.
- Do not say the same thing in every slide.
- Do not create an educational lecture.

## Example Flow

Slide 1:

NEVILLE GODDARD'S STRANGE 7 MINUTE MONEY TECHNIQUE BEFORE SLEEP

Slide 2:

Most people do not have a money problem.

They have a money rehearsal problem.

Slide 3:

Kitchen table version:

Your mind repeats what feels normal.

Make wealth feel normal first.
