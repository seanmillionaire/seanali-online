# Banned Language

## Avoid These Phrases

- unlock your potential
- elevate your life
- embark on a journey
- transform your mindset
- manifestation made easy
- tap into abundance
- quantum leap into success
- unleash your power
- step into greatness
- create the life of your dreams
- high vibration
- limitless potential
- in today's fast-paced world
- game changer
- ultimate guide
- I hope this email finds you well
- Neville mistake
- the promise is not more theory
- stop rehearsing the problem

## Avoid This Style

- Corporate.
- Therapy language.
- Guru language.
- Soft self-help.
- Generic AI tone.
- Long explanations.

## Replace With

Instead of:

Unlock your potential.

Say:

Stop practicing the old version of yourself.

Instead of:

Manifest your dream life.

Say:

Make the future feel normal before it gets here.

Instead of:

Transform your mindset.

Say:

Change the pattern running your decisions.
