# Content Rules

## Main Rule

Every asset must answer:

1. Does this get new customers?
2. Does this increase LTV, AOV, or customer spend?
3. Does this make someone care immediately?
4. Does this feel alive or like a brochure?
5. Is this trend-aware?
6. Is there drama, clickbait, curiosity, villain, proof, belief change, and CTA?

## Every Asset Must Include

- Big outcome.
- Clear pain.
- Curiosity gap.
- Simple belief shift.
- Reason to keep watching.
- Clear next action.
- One natural CTA.

## Sean's Personal Content Preference

Every concept title should include:

- Number.
- Clickbait.
- Outcome.
- Timeframe.
- It must read naturally.

Do not bolt "tonight" onto the end of a title if it sounds bad.

Bad:

3 Reasons People Fail To Manifest Money Tonight

Better:

Neville Goddard's Strange 7 Minute Money Technique Before Sleep

## Winning Content Formula

1. Pattern interrupt.
2. Problem nobody says out loud.
3. New belief.
4. Proof or example.
5. Simple next step.
6. CTA.

## Hook Rules

DO:

- Make it specific.
- Add tension.
- Make the viewer feel exposed.
- Use numbers.
- Use timeframes naturally.
- Name the outcome.
- Make the promise obvious.

DON'T:

- Do not be vague.
- Do not say "unlock your potential."
- Do not use soft self-help language.
- Do not create claims that platforms would reject.
- Do not make the hook smarter than it is clickable.

## Examples

Bad:

Manifest your dream life today.

Better:

Neville Goddard's Strange 7 Minute Money Technique Before Sleep.

Bad:

Change your mindset.

Better:

Your money feeling is not random.

You practiced it.
