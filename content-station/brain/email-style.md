# Email Style

## Email Rules

- Simple subject.
- Clickbait, but not spammy.
- Personal opening.
- One clear idea.
- Short lines.
- Reply bait before link when useful.
- CTA should feel natural.
- No corporate intro.

## Structure

1. Subject.
2. Pattern interrupt.
3. Relatable pain.
4. New belief.
5. Short story or example.
6. Offer or reply bait.
7. CTA.

## Tone

Kitchen table.

Simple.

Direct.

No fake urgency.

No "hope you are well."

## Reply Bait Examples

Reply MONEY FLOW and I will send it over.

Reply RESET if you want the 7-minute audio.

Reply BELIEF if you want the cheat sheet.

Reply SYSTEM if you want the AI income setup.

## Avoid

- Long intros.
- Overexplaining.
- Too many links.
- Fake urgency.
- Robotic greetings.
