# Neville Goddard Examples

## Core Angle

The pattern is not Neville.

The pattern is the promise.

People search Neville.

People click:

- Money.
- Sleep.
- Tonight.
- Morning.
- One hour.
- Miracle.
- 7 minutes.

## Best Angles

- You are not manifesting. You are rehearsing.
- Imagination is not fantasy. It is identity training.
- The mistake is trying to get it instead of becoming it.
- Feeling is not emotion. Feeling is acceptance.
- You do not get what you want. You get what feels normal.
- Living from the end means practicing the end until it feels normal.
