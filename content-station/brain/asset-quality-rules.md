# Asset Quality Rules

## An Asset Is Too Thin If

- It sounds generic.
- It could apply to any brand.
- It has no specific pain.
- It has no curiosity.
- It has no belief shift.
- It has no CTA.
- It sounds like ChatGPT.
- It feels like filler.
- It does not match the selected concept.
- It uses the title, then talks about something else.

## Minimum Standard

Every asset must answer:

1. Who is this for?
2. What pain are they in?
3. What belief are we changing?
4. Why should they care now?
5. What should they do next?
6. How does this move revenue?

## Asset Minimums

Slideshow:

- At least 10 slides.
- Prefer 13 slides.
- Must follow the concept.
- Must include CTA.

Shorts:

- Multiple angles.
- Kitchen-table simple.
- High wow factor.
- Strong claims without fake proof.
- 5th grade readable.

IG Captions:

- At least 3 captions.
- Must not be generic.
- Must match the concept.
- Must include CTA.

Email:

- Short lines.
- One idea.
- Natural CTA.

## Upgrade Examples

Bad:

Believe in yourself.

Better:

You do not need more belief.

You need to stop practicing the version of you that keeps losing.

Bad:

Use AI to make money.

Better:

Most people use AI to make content.

Operators use AI to build assets that bring buyers.
