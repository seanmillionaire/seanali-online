Daily questions

What The Engine Should Answer Every Morning

🔥 What are 20 ad angles worth testing today?

Sorted by desire bucket, offer match, emotional intensity, and speed to produce.

🎬 What are the 10 strongest YouTube hooks?

Not generic titles. Hooks with a clear pain, curiosity gap, and Sean-style point of view.

🎁 What lead magnet should be built next?

Based on active demand, email list fit, and which paid offer it naturally leads to.

🧲 Which emotional themes are trending?

Money fear, AI overwhelm, spiritual doubt, sleep stress, procrastination, or identity pressure.

🧩 Which offer matches the signal?

Money Flow, MoneyWave, Complete Library, Manifestation Genie, AI Freedom System, or a new bundle.

📦 What complete package should we create?

Email, short, landing-page block, image prompt, thumbnail, CTA, and follow-up test.

- Landing-page headline
- CTA
- Image prompt
- thumbnail text
Keep it Sean Ali: simple, direct, emotional, not corporate.
