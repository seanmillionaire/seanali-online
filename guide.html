The 5-Minute Money Visualization Guide

Choose one financial goal, turn it into a scene your mind can actually picture, and decide what you are going to do about it tomorrow.

By the end, you will have:
  • One clear money goal
  • One thought you keep repeating
  • One 10-second scene to visualize tonight
  • One real action to take tomorrow

Do the exercises as you go. Your answers are saved on this device.

01

Why I started doing this

I did not start with some secret advantage.

I grew up in a single-parent home in Canada. Money was tight. At 15, I went through cancer and surgery.

A few years later I discovered online business, meditation and visualization. I became fascinated by a simple question:

The questionWhat if I stopped spending all my mental time picturing what I was afraid would happen and got clearer about what I actually wanted to build?

I still had to learn skills, sell, build websites, run ads, make mistakes and keep going.

Over the next 15+ years, I built businesses, helped generate more than $25M in sales, traveled the world and built a location-independent life in Panama.

Visualization did not do all of that for me. It became one tool I used to keep my mind pointed toward where I wanted to go while I did the real work.

That is what this guide is forNot to escape reality. To get clear on the destination, then move.
02

Pick the money result that would actually help

Do not choose your entire dream life. Pick the next result that would make a real difference.

Example: “I want $10,000 saved” or “I want my business consistently doing $1,000 days.”
03

Catch the sentence already running in your head

You do not need to diagnose yourself. Just catch the sentence.

Maybe it sounds like: “I don't know how.” “I'm already behind.” “Nothing I've tried works.” “I need more money before I can start.” “People like me don't make that much.”
When I think about making or keeping more money, part of me says...

Do not argue with it for ten minutes. You are simply noticing what your mind keeps practicing.

04

Turn the goal into one 10-second scene

Do not picture “being rich.” Picture something you could actually see.

If your goal is savings: picture opening your banking app and seeing $10,247.
If your goal is business growth: picture looking at yesterday's sales and seeing your first $1,000 day.
If your goal is paying off debt: picture opening the account and seeing BALANCE: $0.

One scene. Ten seconds. Something your brain can actually picture.

Write the scene like a snapshot. Where are you? What is on the screen, page or in front of you?
05

Use this simple 5-minute routine tonight

  1. 1Minute 1: Put the phone down. Breathe slowly and let the day settle.
  2. 2Minute 2: Bring up the 10-second scene you just created.
  3. 3Minutes 3–4: Replay it slowly. See what you would see. Hear what you would hear. Notice how it would feel knowing it was handled.
  4. 4Minute 5: Ask, “What is one thing I can do tomorrow to move closer to this?”
Send the message. Publish the offer. Review the numbers. Apply. Save the money. Learn the skill. Make the call.
Keep this groundedYou are not trying to magically create money in five minutes. You are practicing the destination, then connecting it to action.
