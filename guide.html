THE SIMPLE 3-STEP GUIDE

Dream Life. Purpose. Mission.

Big life questions feel easier when you make them small enough to answer today.

🏡

1. Picture a better day

Forget what looks impressive. Imagine an ordinary day you would love to live. Think about who is there, what you do, and how you feel.

Ask yourself: “What do I want more of?”

❤️

2. Notice what matters

Maybe you want more peace, time with family, freedom to create, or meaningful work. Those feelings are clues to what gives your life purpose.

Ask yourself: “Why does that matter to me?”

🧭

3. Choose one small step

Turn the life you pictured into something you can do this week. A phone call, a quiet morning, a new habit, or a first conversation counts.

Ask yourself: “What can I do first?”
