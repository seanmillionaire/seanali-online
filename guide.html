Hey, I'm Sean. If “What do I want from my life?” feels like a big question, let's make it smaller. Start with a day you'd love to live.

I'll walk you through three simple ideas. You can think about your answers as you read, then explore them in my free Dream Life GPS.

You don't need a new career, a big ambition, or your whole life figured out. More peace, closer relationships, better health, or time for something you love is a good place to begin.

01

Dream life: picture a better day.

Set aside what would impress anyone else. Think about an ordinary day that would feel good to you. Who's around? What do you have time for? How do you want to feel?

“What would I like more of in my everyday life?”

It could be: A peaceful morning, a walk with someone you love, work you enjoy, or an afternoon in the garden.

02

Purpose: notice what matters.

Look underneath that picture. Maybe the garden gives you peace. Maybe family time helps you feel connected. Those reasons are clues to what makes life meaningful to you.

“Why does that matter to me?”

You don't have to discover one grand calling. Caring for someone, creating, learning, or bringing a little kindness into the day can matter deeply.

03

Mission: choose a direction for now.

For this guide, think of your mission as how you want to put what matters into practice. Give yourself a direction that fits this season of your life.

“What do I want to make more room for—and what's one small way to begin?”

For example: “I want to feel closer to my family. I'll call my sister this week.” Or, “I want to create again. I'll set aside a little time to paint.”

Your dream life is the picture. Your purpose is why it matters. Your mission is a direction you can act on.

Let your answers be simple. You can change them as you learn more about yourself.
