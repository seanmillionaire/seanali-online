💡 Paid Traffic — Strategic Playbook
THE PAID TRAFFIC
OPERATING RULES
🎯
One Funnel Only
Never send cold paid traffic to multiple offers. All paid traffic goes into one spine: MoneyWave → MoneyFlow. The funnel earns the traffic cost. Everything else is backend.
📊
The Only Number That Matters Early
Cost per email subscriber. Target under $3. If email → sale rate is 5% and AOV is $75, a $3 lead = 2.5x ROAS before any upsells, recurring, or backend.
💡
Ad Creative Research
Scour Reddit and Amazon reviews for real user complaints about competing products. Take those exact pain words and build your ad copy around the solved version. Your ad = their complaint, fixed.
📈
The Scale Signal
Don't scale until you can answer: how much is each lead worth after 7, 30, and 60 days? That LTV number decides your max CPL. Without it, you're guessing at scale.
