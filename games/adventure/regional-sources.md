# Regional visual references

The user-supplied **Panama Regional Field Guide (October 2026)** is the primary
visual brief. Its 17 photographs were inspected as internal references. None
are copied into the game or distributed as textures. Models are original,
compressed interpretations of documented settings, not surveyed replicas of
individual properties. The guide covers more provinces and comarcas than the
current game map; the scenery pass applies the appropriate references to the
existing destinations. It does not invent villages for unrepresented comarcas.

## Verified context used in the game

- Gorgona: mixed light and black volcanic sand, Pacific beach and inland town.
  [Panama Tourism Authority — Pacific Riviera beaches](https://www.tourismpanama.com/blog/post/beaches-to-enjoy-the-riviera-pacifica//)
- Coastal apartments coexist with the smaller town buildings; tower silhouettes
  are generalized, not a reproduction of a named development.
  [Empresas Bern — Royal Palm, Gorgona](https://empresasbern.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/02/Royal-Palm-Espa%C3%B1ol.pdf)
- Bocas: distinguish colorful timber settlement from Bastimentos' forest-backed
  beaches and protected interior. No province-wide clothing or music invented.
  [Panama Tourism Authority — Bocas del Toro](https://www.tourismpanama.com/places-to-visit/bocas-del-toro/)
- Taboga: hillside homes, flowering gardens, tropical dry forest and narrow lanes.
  [Panama Tourism Authority — Taboga](https://es.tourismpanama.com/lugares-para-visitar/isla-taboga/)
- Chiriquí: coffee cultivation and greener highland valleys differ from the bare
  upper mountain. Coffee shrubs are placed around Boquete and Volcán only.
  [Panama Tourism Authority — Boquete](https://www.tourismpanama.com/places-to-visit/boquete/)
- Colón: port districts and Caribbean greenery remain distinct from fort settings.
  [Panama Tourism Authority — Caribbean historic sites](https://www.tourismpanama.com/culture-cuisine/historical-sites/)
- Guna Yala: low pale-sand cays, shallow turquoise water, many uninhabited islands.
  Mainland Guna settings in the guide are not modeled as identical island resorts.
  [Panama Tourism Authority — Caribbean itinerary](https://www.tourismpanama.com/plan-your-trip/itineraries/panama-caribbean-choose-your-nautical-playground/)
- Coiba: tropical moist forest, open beaches and protected marine environment.
  [UNESCO — Coiba](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1138)
- Pearl Islands: varied forested islands, beaches and rocky shores; not every
  island is a resort.
  [Panama Tourism Authority — Las Perlas](https://www.tourismpanama.com/places-to-visit/panama-city/places-to-stay/pearl-islands/)
- Cébaco: Pacific island, forest and surf coast. Existing activities remain.
  [Panama Tourism Authority — Surf](https://es.tourismpanama.com/aire-libre-y-naturaleza/aventura-al-aire-libre/surf/)

## Rendering rules

Keep layered regional greens and differences in sand/water; avoid a global
brown filter. Freshwater color uses the same habitat mask as wildlife, not a
hand-drawn rectangle. Buildings, forests, paths and collision footprints use
shared deterministic plans. Existing save coordinates are retained.
