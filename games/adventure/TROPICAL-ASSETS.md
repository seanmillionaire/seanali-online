# Tropical art upgrade

Created with the built-in image generation tool. `tropical-atlas.png` is one shared transparent texture atlas; `tropical.js` uses its 16 cells for textured vegetation, fruit, and wildlife in the 3D scene. The cutouts are photographic-style generated assets, not scanned 3D models. Terrain, water, buildings, navigation, and the player are rendered in 3D. The atlas is decoded once and shared across objects; textures are disposed when leaving the scene.

## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a single production texture atlas for a tropical island exploration game, square 2048x2048, transparent background. EXACT 4 by 4 equally sized invisible grid, 16 isolated subjects, one centered in each cell with 8 percent transparent padding; no object crosses cell boundaries. Each is a realistic photographic cutout, natural proportions, detailed surfaces, soft neutral daylight, viewed from a slightly elevated three-quarter angle, complete object in frame. Row1 left to right: full tall coconut palm tree with slender textured trunk and spreading fine feathery green fronds; full banana plant with broad torn natural leaves; dense tropical fern; weathered grey limestone boulder. Row2: brown husked coconut; ripe red-yellow mango with leaf; whole pineapple with green crown; small curved bunch of ripe yellow bananas. Row3: realistic green sea turtle seen slightly from above; small red land crab; standing white egret with long neck and black legs; perched keel-billed toucan with realistic feather detail. Row4: yellow blue tropical angelfish side view; orange white clownfish side view; small living branching coral cluster; red hibiscus shrub with green leaves and several flowers. Natural wildlife, NOT cartoon, NOT low-poly, NOT plastic, NOT icons, no labels, text, borders, shadows outside subjects, floor or scenery. Preserve fully transparent space between every subject. Realistic game vegetation and wildlife photography atlas.
