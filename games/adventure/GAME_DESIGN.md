# Panamá Aventura — current local design brief

Audience: young children with a participating adult; short Spanish-first instructions, complete English alternative, mouse/keyboard and large touch controls. Exact age range and unaided comprehension still require a guardian-led playtest.

Fantasy: explore a living Panamá, help people, build a home and choose the next journey.

Pillars: Explore / Make / Connect. Loop: choose one visible action → move or interact → see a result → earn or discover something → save → choose again. Fifteen seconds after the world becomes usable: choose the single story action. Three minutes: walk into the city and use the phone/map. Fifteen minutes: aim to finish the introduction and reach Gorgona. These are design targets, not measured child outcomes.

Style: colorful sculpted 3D architecture, open sightlines, broad silhouettes before small details. Estates use original geometry, three distinct silhouettes, no discovery-spoiling labels. Existing licensed songs remain; narration uses device voices without downloading a voice model. No new chat, accounts, purchases or external child-facing links.
