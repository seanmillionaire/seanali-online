The larger mission

Uplift people.
Uplift the planet.

For many years, I’ve carried one clear intention: help uplift the planet, inspire humans, and help humanity move toward something better.

The mission starts with the human

Change starts close.

I don’t completely understand the full meaning of this mission yet. I’m still uncovering it.

But I know it starts with the human doing their part.

First me. Then my family and immediate environment. Then the people I can reach.

The next phase

Take the message worldwide.

The next step is scale.

My company is not separate from the mission. It gives me a way to distribute ideas, products, and messages far beyond what I could do one person at a time.

Me → Family & immediate environment → Customers → Tens of thousands → Hundreds of thousands → Worldwide

Business as the vehicle

Build the infrastructure. Increase the reach.

Marketing, sales, products, funnels, technology, AI, and paid traffic are the tools I’ve spent years learning.

Paid advertising matters to me because it gives me the ability to intentionally increase distribution.

Me + laptop + company + products + funnels + advertising = distribution at scale

The path connects

Everything led to distribution.

Computers → Internet → Web building → Creating → Marketing → Sales → Funnels → Paid traffic → AI → Distribution at scale

What started as an obsession with the internet eventually gave me a set of tools capable of distributing ideas and products worldwide.

The mission

Uplifting people at scale and, through people, helping uplift the planet.

I’m still discovering exactly what that looks like. But that is the direction.
