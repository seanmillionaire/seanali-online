Money Meditation: The Night One Changed How I Saw My Life

I grew up in Canada in a single-parent home. In my early twenties, one money meditation changed how I saw my future.

By Sean Ali | Published August 31, 2026 | Updated August 31, 2026

Try The 3 Steps

Hey, my name is Sean Ali.

I want to tell you about a money meditation I tried when I was in my early twenties.

But before I tell you what happened that night, you need to know where I came from.

I grew up in Canada in a single-parent home. There was love in my home. There was also pressure. Money was tight. Addiction was around me. I learned what stress felt like when I was still a kid.

Then, when I was 15 years old, I was diagnosed with cancer and had surgery.

That changes the way you look at life.

I understood very young that life is not promised. I also started to understand that one hard part of your life does not have to become your whole life.

I did not have money. I did not have connections. Nobody handed me a secret plan.

What I had was a computer. I loved music. I played with websites. I kept trying to understand this new thing called the internet.

And somewhere inside me, I had a quiet thought: maybe the life I can see around me is not the only life I can live.

🌙 The night my life changed

When I was in my early twenties, I experimented with a money meditation.

That night my life changed.

Now listen, I want to explain that carefully.

I did not open my eyes and find money on the floor. I did not wake up rich the next morning. Nobody knocked on my door with a cheque.

The room was the same.

I was the part that had changed.

For a few minutes, I saw a life in my mind that I had never seen around me growing up.

I saw myself making money online. I saw myself building businesses from my laptop. I pictured myself travelling. I pictured waking up in a place I chose, doing work I had built, instead of living a life somebody else had chosen for me.

For the first time, that life felt like a real place I could walk toward.

Think about this for a second.

If you have only seen one kind of life, how are you supposed to know another kind is possible?

That was the gift of that meditation. It let me see beyond what I already knew.

💡 The room was the same. I was the part that had changed.

🤔 So what did the money meditation really do?

It did not give me money.

It helped me learn a new rule about money.

The old rule was simple: people like us struggle. Money is hard to get. Freedom belongs to somebody else.

I did not sit down one day and choose those thoughts. I learned them by watching life around me.

This is what I mean when I talk about a belief.

If I were explaining it to my daughter, I would say this: a belief is a thought you have heard, seen, or repeated so many times that you stop asking if it is true.

Maybe a child hears, "We cannot afford that," every week.

Maybe that child watches the adults around them worry each time a bill arrives.

Years later, the child is grown. The words are no longer being said out loud. But the feeling is still there each time money comes up.

That is why a person can want more money and still do things like this:

  • 💰 Avoid looking at their bank account.
  • 💼 Want a better job but feel afraid to ask for one.
  • 🤝 Build a business but feel guilty when it is time to charge.

Nothing is wrong with them.

They may simply be following rules they learned a long time ago.

A money meditation gives you a quiet place to hear those rules.

Imagine a television has been playing in the next room all day. At first you notice it. After a while, you forget it is on. Then someone turns it off and the whole house feels different.

That is what quiet time can do. It helps you hear the words that have been playing in the background of your own mind.

Then you can ask a better question: is this true, or is this just what I learned?

That night, I began to see money as something I could learn to create, receive, manage, and use well.

But I still had to get up the next morning and do something.

At 18, I made my first online sale. It was a small sale, but it showed me that I could create something in one place and sell it to a person somewhere else in the world.

So I kept learning. I built websites. I learned marketing. I made offers. I bought ads. I lost money. I made mistakes. I fixed what I could and kept going.

Over more than 15 years, that work helped me generate over $25 million in sales, travel the world, and build a location-independent life in Panama.

The meditation did not build that life for me.

It helped me see where I wanted to go. Then my choices had to carry me there.

Over the years, I started making my own money audios. Money Flow came from that work. It is a three-part guided hypnosis meditation system designed to support subconscious reprogramming around money, receiving, and abundance.

In plain words, you listen to the audios and practice different thoughts about money.

It is a tool. It is not a promise that money will appear.

🔍 What research can honestly say

I want to be honest with you because people say a lot of wild things when money and spirituality are put together.

Research can study meditation, attention, stress, thoughts, and behavior. A meta-analysis of 111 randomized trials found small to moderate effects from mindfulness programs on several parts of thinking, including sustained attention and working memory accuracy.

Another review of 47 trials found moderate evidence that mindfulness meditation programs can reduce anxiety, depression, and pain in some clinical groups. It also found weak or insufficient evidence for several other outcomes.

Neither paper says meditation attracts money. Neither paper says a thought sends a bank deposit into your life.

They support a smaller and more honest point: meditation can be a useful way to practice attention. What you do with that attention is still up to you.

✅ Fact

Meditation can train attention. Research does not show that it makes money appear.

💡 Meaning

I use money meditation to notice my thoughts and practice more useful ones.

🙏 Faith

You may believe God, prayer, or universal laws also matter. That is belief, not laboratory proof.
