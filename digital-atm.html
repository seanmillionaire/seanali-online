Common questions

Before You Start

Do I need technical experience?

No. The lessons use plain language, readable screen zooms, and one task per lesson. You should be comfortable using basic websites and apps on your phone.

Do I need to film myself or build a following?

No. This is not a selfie-video, influencer, or personal-brand course. The process focuses on answering real buyer questions where useful recommendations are permitted.

Do I need to buy inventory or ship products?

No. The system teaches recommendation commissions through third-party retailer or affiliate programs. The retailer handles the product transaction and fulfillment.

How much time does the daily routine take?

The target routine is 15–20 focused minutes. Initial account setup and the first walkthrough take longer, but the repeatable daily activity is intentionally small.

Does AI do everything for me?

No. AI helps analyze buyer intent, match options, and draft clear recommendations. You still verify facts, edit the answer, follow community rules, disclose tracked links when required, and track the results.

How quickly will I earn a commission?

There is no guaranteed timeline. Results depend on program approval, the questions you select, product fit, traffic, your recommendations, retailer conversion, and consistent execution. The course gives you a troubleshooting process so you can improve from real data.

Is this a proprietary Digital ATM app?

No. “Digital ATM” is the name of the guided process. You receive training, templates, prompts, checklists, walkthroughs, and a tracker—not software that automatically generates earnings.
