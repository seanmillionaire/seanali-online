04 / TURN THE BUILD INTO DISTRIBUTION

Get it out there.

One offer. Three honest angles. One measurable next action.

01

Show the product

Capture the real app, gameplay or audio experience. Open on the useful moment. Make the first frame understandable without a long explanation.

02

Show the human need

Use a real customer question or permissioned experience. Explain the problem it addresses. Never fabricate a buyer, testimonial or result.

03

Show the founder

Sean explains why this exists, who it helps and what to try first. Use a true experience and one direct link to the matching offer.

  1. Before launch

    Verify the phone journey, price, checkout, delivery and tracking. Choose one outcome: useful session, lead, play completion or purchase.

  2. First seven days

    Prepare native Facebook, Instagram and YouTube variations for the right brand. Start with the SOP’s 1–2 useful posts per active brand per day only where quality and access support it. Publishing remains a separate approved action.

  3. Learn at 24h, 72h and 7d

    Record each post URL, reach, saves, shares, qualified clicks and the target conversion. Compare multiple posts, not one lucky spike. Keep phone posting as a benchmark when testing a scheduler.

  4. Amplify what earns it

    Test paid distribution only with an approved spend cap and working measurement. Use Ads Manager; keep engagement and purchase tests separate. Scale on buyer outcomes and contribution profit.

The decision is the deliverable.

After each bounded test: SCALE, TEST, FIX, HOLD or KILL. Record the result, what changed your mind and the next action. Leave paused systems paused.
