Here’s why I made it

I tell the true story behind the offer, what I learned and why it matters. Then I show the person what they can try next.

  1. First: make the next click work

    I open the page on my phone, check the offer and follow the buying or sign-up path. I choose what I want the visitor to do and make sure I can measure it.

  2. Then: prepare something worth sharing

    I turn the three angles above into short videos or clear image posts for the right brand’s Facebook, Instagram and YouTube. My starting target is 1–2 useful posts a day per active brand, only when I can keep the quality up. I review the content before it goes out.

  3. Next: stay in the conversation

    I answer real questions, join relevant conversations and help people take the next step. I can send a matching offer to an interested email segment without sending every offer to everyone. I keep the link, clicks and results together.

  4. After 1, 3 and 7 days: see what worked

    I check shares, useful replies, visits and buyers. If something attracts the right people, I can choose a capped Ads Manager test. I compare what it earns with what it costs before adding more money.

My rule: give the winners more room.

I grow what works, fix what is blocking it and park what does not deserve more time. Each test should leave me with one clear decision about what I do next.
